Block Stack

Choose a mode to start. Arrow keys move, Up or Z rotates, Space drops, P pauses, M mutes.

Special pieces (Special mode)

💣 Bomb: explodes on landing or with X, wiping blocks within 3 cells. Special pieces have their own colour and are named in the Next box and above it while falling. ⚡ Lightning: lands like a normal piece, then deletes every column it occupies. 👻 Ghost: falls straight through the stack. When it settles, each of its blocks fills the lowest empty hole in its column, including cavities buried under other blocks. Space settles it right away; a block whose column is full is lost. 🧲 Snapper: also falls through the stack, but each block only fills the top surface, landing on the highest block of its column and ignoring buried holes. R: Time Rewind, once per game, undoes the last 3 pieces and restores the next-piece queue.
